Who we are

Built for Mining. Trusted Across Zambia.

Established in 2012, MPK Mining Equipment & Supplies Ltd is a privately owned Zambian company specializing in the wholesale distribution and retail supply of mining equipment, industrial hardware, automotive consumables, and engineering solutions.

Headquartered in Ndola's Light Industrial Area, we proudly support mining companies, contractors, transport operators, and industrial businesses with high quality products sourced from globally recognized manufacturers.

Our experienced technical, procurement, and logistics teams manage every stage of the supply chain, from international sourcing and import coordination to on time delivery and after sales support, ensuring our customers receive the right products exactly when they need them.
