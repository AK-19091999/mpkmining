Who we are

Local footing, international sourcing.

MPK Mining Equipment & Supplies Ltd is based in the Light Industrial Area of Ndola, at the heart of Zambia's Copperbelt. We work with mines, contractors and fleet operators who cannot afford downtime — sourcing off-the-road tyres, machinery parts and industrial consumables from manufacturers across India, the UAE and China, and managing the import process end to end.

The team is small by design: technical staff who understand equipment specification, and a sales and logistics group who track every shipment from purchase order to delivery on site.
